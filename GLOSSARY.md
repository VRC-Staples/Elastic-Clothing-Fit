# Elastic Clothing Fit

Elastic Clothing Fit changes clothing geometry to fit a body while the user adjusts a preview.

## Language

- Fitted vertex: A clothing vertex that receives direct fit displacement.
- Preserved vertex: A clothing vertex excluded from direct fit displacement. Preserve-follow can still move this vertex.
- Frozen vertex: A clothing vertex outside the selected groups in exclusive fitting. Fit controls do not move this vertex.
- Prepared fit: The original geometry and fit displacements for one preview. Preview controls recalculate positions from this data.
- Preserve-follow: Movement of preserved vertices based on nearby fitted vertices. Group offset changes do not contribute to this movement.
- Update attempt: One selected add-on release and its download.
- Verified archive: A downloaded add-on archive that matches its advertised SHA-256 value.
